Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. How does the engagement process work?

A1. Contact us through any of the methods listed on Contatti, describe your requirements, and you will receive an estimated quotation. If you agree to proceed, the Provider will issue a Contract for your review and signature.

Q2. What is the initial Project Blueprint fee?

A2. To initiate the process, the Client must pay EUR 130, or the equivalent amount in another agreed currency. Upon receipt of this payment, the Provider will issue the first Project Blueprint, which will define the technical specifications of the project and the applicable installment schedule for the Client's review and approval.

Q3. When does work begin?

A3. Work will commence only after both the Contract and the corresponding Project Blueprint have been signed. Each signed Project Blueprint constitutes the Client's acceptance of its contents and supersedes any previously issued Project Blueprint or related project document to the extent that they are inconsistent with it.

Q4. How are milestones and installments handled?

A4. Work is performed according to the milestones specified in the applicable Project Blueprint. Upon completion of each milestone, work will pause while the Provider awaits payment of the full installment associated with the next milestone. Once the applicable installment has been received in full, the Provider will resume work on the corresponding milestone.

Q5. What happens if the Client requests that the work be paused?

A5. If the Client requests that work be paused, the Provider may issue a new Project Blueprint for the continuation or reissuance of the project. Each new Project Blueprint is subject to the same EUR 130 Project Blueprint fee, or the equivalent amount in another agreed currency. The Client may refuse a proposed Project Blueprint up to a maximum of three times. Once a Project Blueprint is signed, it is deemed accepted by the Client, and any previous Project Blueprints or related documents concerning the same scope of work are deemed superseded and invalid to the extent that they conflict with the signed Project Blueprint.

Q6. What are the accepted currencies?

A6. We accept the following currencies:

  • EUR (€)
  • US Dollar ($)
  • JPY (¥, Japanese Yen)
  • RON (Romanian leu)
  • ETH (Ethereum)

Prices denominated in mETH are converted to their equivalent ETH amount at the stated rate of 1 mETH = 0.001 ETH.

Prices listed on this website are indicative and may not reflect the final amount payable.

Q7. What increases the price?

  • Windows support (+€200)
  • Linux support added after development has begun as Windows-only (+€100)
  • Web integrations (+€100 per interaction)
  • Database integrations (+€150 per interaction)
  • Database schema creation (+€300)
  • Localization support (+€50), plus €5 per localized phrase

Example: A warehouse stores entries on paper and needs a system to insert products, view their availability, and enter invoices. If it requires only Windows support and English, the final amount due may be €2,400 or 960 mETH.
