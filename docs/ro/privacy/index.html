2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed in connection with this website, products and services, business relationships, contracts, communications and compliance activities.

Depending on the circumstances and on how a person interacts with the controller, the following categories of personal data may be processed:

  • Identification and contact data, such as name, email address, postal address, telephone number, professional position and organisation;
  • Communication data, including the contents of emails, messages, enquiries, support requests and other correspondence;
  • Contractual and commercial data, including information necessary to negotiate, enter into and perform contracts, provide services, manage customers and suppliers, process orders and maintain business records;
  • Financial and accounting data, including information necessary for invoicing, payments, accounting, taxation and related legal obligations;

Personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • obtaining aggregate statistics concerning the use of this website; and
  • improving the website, products, services and business operations.

5. Legal Bases

Depending on the particular processing activity, personal data may be processed on the following legal bases under Article 6 GDPR:

  • Consent — Article 6(1)(a): where the data subject has given consent to processing for one or more specific purposes.
  • Contract — Article 6(1)(b): where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with the data subject or to take steps at the data subject's request before entering into a contract.
  • Legal obligation — Article 6(1)(c): where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation applicable to the controller.
  • Vital interests — Article 6(1)(d): where processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of the data subject or another natural person, where applicable.
  • Public interest — Article 6(1)(e): where processing is necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority, where applicable.
  • Legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f): where processing is necessary for legitimate interests pursued by the controller or a third party, except where those interests are overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject.

The legitimate interests pursued by the controller may include operating and securing the website and business, maintaining appropriate business records, preventing abuse and fraud, communicating with customers and counterparties, improving services, understanding aggregate website usage, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Information about GitHub's processing of personal data is available in GitHub's privacy documentation: GitHub Privacy Statement .

Certain services operated by or on behalf of the controller may transmit information to infrastructure located on or accessible through a separate remote server used for processing or storage.

7. Website Analytics

This website uses GoatCounter to obtain aggregate statistics about website usage.

Depending on the information provided by the service, analytics may include information such as page views, referring websites, browser characteristics, operating-system characteristics, language and approximate geographic information.

GoatCounter is used as a privacy-oriented analytics service. It is designed to avoid retaining raw source information such as IP addresses or complete User-Agent strings for the purpose of identifying individual visitors, and instead stores computed or aggregate information.

The controller uses this information to understand aggregate website usage and to improve the website and its content.

The legal basis relied upon for this processing is the legitimate interest of the controller under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Further information about GoatCounter's processing is available at: GoatCounter Privacy .

Where a technology requiring prior consent under applicable law is introduced, the relevant information and consent mechanism will be provided before the relevant processing takes place.
